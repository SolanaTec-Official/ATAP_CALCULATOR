Solar ATAP Bill Calculator

Estimate your TNB bill under the Solar ATAP scheme.

📘 New here? Read this first — 4 easy steps
1

Get your latest TNB bill

Open the MyTNB app, then select your latest TNB bill.

2

Get your Solar Generation from the Solis app

Open the SolisCloud app → your Station → Overview. Tap the Year view tab, then find the month that matches your latest TNB bill. The kWh figure shown for that month is your Solar Generation (kWh). Example: Year view → Jul → 431 kWh

3

Find the AFA rate on your TNB bill

Open your latest TNB bill. In the Bill Details section, look for the row with the label "AFA".

4

Fill in the form and press Calculate

Choose your tariff below, type in the numbers you just found, leave Battery Charge/Discharge at 0 if you don't have a battery, and press the blue Calculate ATAP Bill button. Every field also has a small ⓘ link under it if you need a reminder of where that number comes from.
